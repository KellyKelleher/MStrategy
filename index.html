MStrategy Solutions & Capabilities

Specialized Capabilities Deployed Across Your Portals

Beyond the five core products, MStrategy provides specialized capabilities that can be deployed within its portal solutions to address security, governance and business process requirements.

RBAC — Role-Based Access Control

What it does

Controls access based on a user's assigned role, responsibilities or organizational group.

Customer Value

Ensures the right people access the right information.

ABAC — Attribute-Based Access Control

What it does

Applies dynamic access policies using user, document, organizational or contextual attributes.

Customer Value

Provides granular, flexible security beyond traditional folder permissions.

DRM — Digital Rights Management

What it does

Adds controls such as watermarking, download restrictions and supported document-use protections.

Customer Value

Helps protect sensitive intellectual property and confidential information.

Engage & Collaborate

What it does

Branded experiences, messaging, notifications, forms, checklists, tasks and signing.

Customer Value

Improves user engagement and simplifies collaboration.

Automate & Integrate

What it does

Business workflows, Box automation, APIs, third-party integrations and AI-enabled experiences.

Customer Value

Reduces manual processes and improves operational efficiency.

Monitor & Govern

What it does

Activity reporting, analytics, audit trails, access visibility and administrative controls.

Customer Value

Strengthens oversight, accountability and compliance processes.
