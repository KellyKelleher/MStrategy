Turn Box Into a Business Application Platform

Most organizations use Box to manage content. M Strategy helps organizations turn that content into business outcomes by adding branded portals, workflow automation, forms, dashboards, tasks, and notifications.

2

Secure Access for Every User & Role

Extend Box security with advanced identity management and granular role-based access controls that simplify administration while protecting sensitive information without exposing internal Box structures.

Purpose-Built Solutions for Every Industry

Transform Box into client portals, virtual data rooms, onboarding hubs, or regulated document workspaces designed around real-world processes—not generic file repositories.

Maximize Return on Your Box Investment
